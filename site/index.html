How it works

01

Run everything in parallel

Every task gets its own worktree and branch. Run five agents and your own editor at once, with live terminals side by side in the workbench.

02

Observe, don't ask

Seralith snapshots the tree around every session. Attribution is measured, not self-reported, down to the line. Your own edits are attributed to you.

03

Sign it into history

Each commit carries an in-toto attestation, Ed25519-signed and stored in git notes, recording the tool, the declared model, and who was accountable.

04

Gate it at merge

CI re-verifies everything and enforces policy: no agent edits to pipelines, and security sign-off for agent changes to crypto. Approvals are signed and role-bound.

No token middleman. Ever.

Seralith is not an AI product. It launches your coding tools as ordinary processes: hosted frontier models, local open-weights models, or none at all. We never see your prompts, proxy your traffic, or hold your keys. That is what makes Seralith acceptable inside an enclave.

  • Works with any terminal coding agent, shell, or editor
  • Fully offline and air-gap capable
  • Open, verifiable format. Your history is yours even without us.
