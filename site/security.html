Security

Seralith is security infrastructure. We treat every report with urgency, and we will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@seralith.com. Do not open a public issue. Please include:

  • The affected component (CLI, daemon, workbench, or Seralith Control) and version
  • Reproduction steps or a proof of concept
  • Your assessment of impact, especially any way to forge, strip, or replay attestations, or to bypass a policy gate

Our commitments

StageTarget
Acknowledgement1 business day
Triage and severity3 business days
Fix for critical issues14 days
Coordinated disclosure90 days, or on fix release, whichever is first

We credit reporters in release notes unless they prefer to remain anonymous.

Scope

In scope: signature verification, ledger integrity, policy evaluation, gate bypasses, privilege escalation in the daemon, authentication in Seralith Control, and the integrity of our release artifacts and this website.

Out of scope: denial of service through resource exhaustion on a machine the attacker already controls, issues in third-party coding tools that Seralith launches but does not ship, and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
